← Back to Teaching Ideas

About the Assessment

This assessment was created as a culminating activity for Jason Reynolds’s Long Way Down. Rather than focusing primarily on plot recall, the questions ask students to apply the reading skills practiced throughout the novel, including characterization, inference, theme, symbolism, structure, author’s purpose, irony, and the use of evidence to understand a text.

The assessment includes 20 multiple-choice questions and one student-selected short response. The multiple-choice questions use clear, accessible language while still asking students to analyze the text and Reynolds’s choices as an author. The short-response section gives students three options, allowing them to demonstrate their understanding through application, character analysis, or theme.

The goal was to create a meaningful but accessible summative assessment that measures what students understand about the novel while also reinforcing the types of reading and thinking skills they will need beyond this unit.

This page includes the student assessment, answer sheet, and an explanation of the thinking behind the assessment design so that other teachers can use, adapt, or build from the idea.
