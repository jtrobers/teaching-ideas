Welcome

A Place to Share the Work

Teaching is a constant process of trying something, seeing what happens, and deciding what to keep, revise, or rethink.

This site is a place for me to share lessons, activities, resources, and ideas that have grown out of real classroom planning and instruction. Some are things that worked well. Others are still developing.

The goal is not to present one perfect way to teach something. It is to share materials and thinking that another teacher might be able to use, adapt, question, or improve for their own students.

Lesson 01

Teaching Long Way Down: The Elevator Investigation

A paper-based approach to Jason Reynolds' novel that turns each elevator floor into an investigation. Students collect evidence, analyze character and perspective, examine author's craft, and build toward a final elevator report.
